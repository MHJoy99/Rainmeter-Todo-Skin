## 2. LinkedIn post

Paragraph 1:
If you run Rainmeter on Windows and live inside Google Tasks, this project will save you a few dozen clicks a day. MHJoy99/Rainmeter-Todo-Skin is an open-source, MIT-licensed skin that puts your actual Google Tasks list directly on your desktop. There are no copies and no middle layers: what you see on the skin is the real task in your Google account.

Paragraph 2:
The details matter. Clicking any row opens that real Google Task in your browser, so nothing gets lost in translation. When a task has a date, it automatically becomes an all-day task on that day, matching how you schedule work in Google Tasks on mobile or web. Authentication uses OAuth 2.0, so credentials never touch the skin, and all data is stored locally, meaning the skin keeps working even when you are offline or when API access is interrupted.

Paragraph 3:
The current release is v1.4.6 with a cleaner visual design and more reliable syncing. It is free, open source under the MIT license, and ready for daily use by Windows power users and developers who want their task list one glance away. ## 3. Facebook post

Casual post for a PC/tech group:

Found a cool little project for anyone running Rainmeter on Windows. It puts your Google Tasks right on your desktop, and here is the best part: it is the real Google Tasks list, not a separate copy. You click a todo and it opens the actual task. If you give a task a date, it becomes an all-day task for that day, just like in the Google Tasks app on your phone.

It uses OAuth 2.0 to log in, so your Google password is never saved anywhere, and all the data is stored locally. Even if your internet drops, the skin still works. It is free and open source (MIT license), currently at v1.4.6 with a cleaner look.
